You're Not Alone

Teaching at CT State Community College means you're part of a supportive network of colleagues, administrators, and staff committed to student success. This page connects you to professional development opportunities, key contacts, and campus resources to support your work in ENG 1010.

📝 Practical Information

Payroll & HR (for Adjunct Faculty)

Key Info for Adjuncts

  • Paychecks: Direct deposit (set up through HR)
  • Pay schedule: Bi-weekly (check CT State payroll calendar)
  • Course load limits: Maximum 9 credits per semester for adjuncts
  • Benefits: Limited benefits for part-time faculty; contact HR for details
  • Union representation: Adjunct faculty are represented by a union (varies by collective bargaining agreement)

HR Contact: Check your campus HR office for specific questions

Parking & Campus Access

  • Parking passes: Obtain from Campus Security or Parking Office (varies by campus)
  • Building access: You may need a key or access card for your office/classroom building (request through Department Chair)
  • ID badges: Faculty ID for library access, campus services (obtain at start of employment)

Office Space & Facilities

For Full-Time Faculty

Private office assigned by Department Chair, with computer, phone, desk, filing cabinet

For Adjunct Faculty

Shared adjunct offices: Available on most campuses with desk space, computer access, and filing space. Ask your Department Chair for location and access.

Office hours: Can be held in adjunct office, library, student center, or virtually (Zoom, Blackboard Collaborate)

Printing & Copying

  • Departmental copier: Use for syllabus, handouts (ask Department Chair for access code)
  • Copy limits: Vary by campus; ask your Chair about departmental copying budgets
  • Print shop: Some campuses have print shops for large jobs (course packets, etc.)
💡 Going Paperless: Consider using Blackboard for all course materials to reduce printing costs and increase accessibility. Post syllabus, assignment sheets, readings, and handouts digitally.

💚 Faculty Wellness & Self-Care

Teaching is rewarding but also demanding. Taking care of yourself is essential for sustaining your work with students.

Work-Life Balance Tips

Set Boundaries

Establish clear office hours and stick to them. You don't need to be available 24/7. Set an email auto-reply explaining when you'll respond (e.g., "I check email Monday-Friday 9am-5pm").

Manage Grading Load

Use time-saving strategies from the Assessment page: comment banks, rubrics, focused feedback. Grade in batches with breaks.

Connect with Colleagues

Build relationships with other faculty. Share teaching ideas, vent frustrations, celebrate successes. Teaching is less isolating when you have a community.

Use Campus Resources

Faculty have access to the same wellness resources as students. If you're struggling with stress, anxiety, or burnout, reach out to campus Wellness Services or your EAP (Employee Assistance Program).

ℹ️ Remember: You can't pour from an empty cup. Taking care of your own wellbeing isn't selfish—it's necessary for being present for your students.
