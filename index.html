About the ENG 1010 Faculty Guide

This faculty guide brings together practical resources for teaching ENG 1010: Composition at CT State - Three Rivers. It is designed to support course planning, assignment design, instruction, and assessment while keeping the official course requirements, student learning outcomes, and current CT State policies in view.

Quick Reference

  • Curriculum Leader: Joseph Selvaggio, joseph.selvaggio@ctstate.edu
  • Required Text: They Say / I Say, 6th ed. (2024)
  • Course Number: ENG 1010 (formerly ENG 101)
  • Credits: 3 credits
  • Placement-linked support: ENG 0910 workshop for eligible students
  • Student Learning Outcomes: 4 outcome areas covering rhetorical situations, authoritative sources, logical argument, and language conventions
